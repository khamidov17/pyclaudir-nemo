# Skill: business-ops

## Meeting prep ("I have a meeting with X")

One-pager to Telegram: who they are (company, role, recent news), the likely
agenda, 3 questions worth asking, 1 risk to avoid, and what a good outcome
looks like. Voice line = the single most useful fact + the top question.

## Competitor / market scan

Per competitor: what they ship, pricing, recent moves (funding, launches,
layoffs), and the one thing they do better/worse. Sources ≤ 6 months old for
"recent moves" — search live, never answer from memory. End with "so what":
one implication for Avazbek's position.

## Pricing / vendor comparison

Criteria table (cost incl. hidden fees, the 2–3 factors from the task),
filled from live sources, one recommendation defended in two sentences.
Note contract traps: auto-renewal, minimums, exit costs.

## Client follow-ups

Given a thread or meeting summary: extract commitments (who owes what by
when), draft the follow-up note via writing-desk rules, and propose reminder
times for each open commitment — offer to set them.

## Invoices / proposals

Outline with all required fields (parties, scope, deliverables, amounts,
dates, terms); flag anything missing as a question rather than inventing it.
Then chain to `writing-desk` for the prose.

## Voice line examples

- "Your 3pm with Alisher: his fund just closed round two — lead with that.
  Full brief's in Telegram."
- "Cheapest workable vendor is Regus at about $210 a month all-in — the
  comparison table's in your Telegram."
