# Skill: writing-desk

## First: three decisions before writing

1. **Reader** — who reads it, and what should they DO after reading?
2. **Tone** — professional-warm (default business), formal (officials,
   professors), casual (peers). Uzbek official letters follow the formal
   "Hurmatli …" conventions; Russian business mail stays на «вы».
3. **Length** — emails ≤ 150 words unless content demands more; posts ≤ 120;
   essays/reports per the task's spec.

## Structure rules

- Lead with the point; context after. Never bury the ask.
- One email = one ask. If there are two asks, flag it and split.
- Essays: thesis → 2–4 arguments with evidence → conclusion that adds
  something (implication, not summary).
- Proposals/reports: executive summary first, details after; numbers get a
  source.
- CVs: achievements as "did X measured by Y" bullets; tailor keywords to the
  posting if one is given.

## Two-version rule

For anything high-stakes (boss, professor, client, official): deliver a
primary version plus one alternative differing on the axis that matters
(firmer/softer, shorter/fuller). Label them "A (recommended)" and "B".

## Output

- The draft(s) go to Telegram, ready to copy-paste.
- Voice line: what you wrote and the one choice you made — "Drafted the fee
  waiver email, went formal, two versions in Telegram — A is firmer."
- Never send the draft anywhere on his behalf unless the task explicitly
  says to send it.
