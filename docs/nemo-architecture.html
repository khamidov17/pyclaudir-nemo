🐟 How Nemo Works

A private, proactive voice assistant that lives on your phone — remembers everything, acts on the phone, and reaches out first instead of only answering.

1 The big picture

Nemo is three cooperating pieces + one shared memory. The phone is what you talk to; the brain reasons and schedules; the voice layer makes it fast and natural.

📱 Phone app (Flutter)

What you see and talk to. Hears the wake word, runs the live call, drives the phone, plays Nemo's voice.

Wake word "hey nemo" (on-device) Voice session (mic ↔ speaker) Phone control Chat + voice UI
↑↓ voice over wss · chat over wss · "speak this" pushes

🗣️ nemo-voice

The realtime voice layer (port 3002).

Qwen Omni Realtime (speech→speech) voice_brain (identity + tools)

🧠 pyclaudir engine

The brain + proactive scheduler (port 8765).

Claude Code (reasoning) Reminder loop + webhooks app_api (link to phone)
↓ both read & write the same memory

💾 Shared memory (data/)

One source of truth — anything you tell voice-Nemo, text-Nemo also knows.

memories/*.md + ABOUT_ME voice_journal.jsonl memory_index.db (semantic) pyclaudir.db (history + reminders)

☁️ External services (all China-friendly)

Qwen Omni Realtime (voice) DashScope embeddings (memory) Edge TTS (proactive speech)

2 Voice — how a conversation flows

Native speech-to-speech: audio in → audio out, one model (like Gemini Live, but works in China). No separate transcribe-then-speak — that's why it's fast and natural.

1
"Hey Nemo" — detected on-device (no audio leaves the phone)
2
Mic opens — the live session starts; your speech streams to nemo-voice
3
Qwen understands + acts — calls tools as needed (weather, set reminder, open an app…)
4
Nemo speaks back — natural voice; mic is muted while he talks so he can't cut himself off
5
🔊 "It's 34°, and I'll nudge you at 2 to call Aziz."

3 Memory — how Nemo knows you

Three layers, shared between voice and text. Recall works by meaning, not keywords.

📝
Factsmemories/*.md + your ABOUT_ME profile — curated, durable. Written when you say "remember this".
📜
Journalvoice_journal.jsonl — every spoken turn, so nothing is lost.
🔎
Semantic indexmemory_index.db — embeds everything so "what coffee do I drink?" finds "dark roast" with no shared words.

A nightly self-reflection + weekly consolidation keep memory tidy and refresh your profile automatically.

6 Privacy & security

  • 📱
    App-only — no Telegram dependency; the phone is the only interface. Wake word & all audio capture are on-device.
  • 🙈
    Read-gating — Nemo can never read your screen, messages, or camera on its own or during a reminder. Only when you explicitly ask, in that moment.
  • 🔐
    Biometric gate on sensitive actions (camera, typing).
  • 🛡️
    Encrypted transport — TLS with a pinned certificate; app updates are signature- + hash-verified.

💡 The idea: Nemo is built in layers — a fast realtime voice on top, a reasoning brain underneath, one shared memory in the middle, and a proactive loop that lets him speak first. Each piece is small and swappable: the voice model, the wake word, and individual skills can all change without touching the rest.
